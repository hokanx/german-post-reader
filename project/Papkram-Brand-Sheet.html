03 — Methodology

The DECODE Protocol™

Our named 5-step method — internally A.C.T.R.E. — mirrors exactly what happens inside the product, from upload to sent reply. Lead with DECODE in marketing; each step name should sound like progress, not process.

Upload

02 · CLASSIFY

04 · REVEAL DEADLINES

Surface the Frist

05 · EXECUTE REPLY

Hand over a ready German reply

04 — Personality & voice

Calm expert. Plain words.
Zero condescension.

Papkram talks like the knowledgeable friend who reads the scary letter for you and says, “Here's what they want, here's why it matters, here's what you do.” Reassuring, precise, and always on the reader's side of the screen.

Reassuring

Lower the temperature. Most letters are less dangerous than they feel.

Precise

Name the action, the date, the amount. Specific beats clever.

Direct

Short active sentences. “Send this reply,” not “A reply may be submitted.”

On your side

Never make the reader feel small. It's not your fault, and it's not your job to decode.

DO

  • Say what the letter means and what to do.
  • Lead with the deadline and the consequence.
  • Use plain English (and the user's own language).
  • Keep German for the reply — never for the reader.
  • Frame progress: Reveal, Execute, Resolve, Done.

DON'T

  • Call it “translation.” We decode, we don't translate.
  • Bury the action inside legal or product jargon.
  • Use fear as the message — name it, then dissolve it.
  • Over-promise legal advice. We clarify and draft.
  • Make the reader feel like a burden for not knowing.

06 — Logo & mark

The envelope, made primary

The identity is the amber “sticker” envelope from the product itself — a rounded tile with a 1.5px ink outline, a hard offset shadow, and a white envelope, set beside Papkram in ink. It's warm, tactile and unmistakably the app. It replaces the earlier document/speech-bubble mark as the single primary logo.

Primary lockup — on paper Reversed — on ink
Brand icon
Store tile
Stacked
Construction
  • Amber tile (gradient #FB9A4B→#F07E31), corner radius ≈ 30%.
  • Ink outline 1.5px + hard ink shadow offset down-right — the house “sticker” style.
  • White envelope with ink outline centred inside.
  • Wordmark: Papkram, sentence case, Sora ExtraBold in ink.
  • Brand icon = sticker (outline + shadow). Store tile = full-bleed, no shadow.

CLEAR SPACE & USE

  • Keep clear space ≥ the height of the icon tile on all sides.
  • Minimum icon size 24 px; minimum lockup width 130 px.
  • Use the sticker icon on light UI; the full-bleed tile for app-store & favicons.
  • Reversed lockup on ink or photos; keep the amber tile — it's the brand pop.

MISUSE

  • Don't recolour the tile away from amber or drop the ink outline.
  • Don't set “Papkram” in all-caps, condense it, or add a second colour.
  • Don't keep the hard shadow on the full-bleed app-store tile.
  • Don't place the low-contrast lockup directly on busy imagery.

GRADIENTS

Papier → Peach

Soft radial on light surfaces (top-right light source). Ink → Violet on dark hero bands.

CONTRAST

Accessible pairings

Ink on Cream and white passes AA/AAA. Amber is a surface/accent colour — set text on amber in Ink, never white text on amber.
